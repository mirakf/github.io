🎓 Academic Appointments#

My ultimate goal is to join a teaching-forward college or university as a faculty member in geography, environmental science, earth science, or a related field. I am actively on the academic job market and available for positions beginning as early as Fall 2027.

Teaching Philosophy#

Science belongs to everyone — and so do its benefits. My teaching is grounded in the belief that rigorous, accessible science creates better outcomes for communities locally and globally. In the classroom I emphasize hands-on learning, real-world applications, and the kind of critical thinking that travels with students long after they leave my course.

🛠️ Consulting Services#

My research expertise translates directly into practical skills for organizations working at the intersection of conservation, data science, and environmental planning. I offer consulting services to NGOs, government agencies, academic institutions, and private organizations.

What I Offer#

🗺️ Custom Ecosystem and Land Cover Mapping Reproducible, satellite-derived ecosystem maps tailored to your study area and conservation or planning needs. My work in Belize demonstrates this capability at the national scale across 27 ecosystem types and 11 years of annual data.

📡 Remote Sensing Analysis Multi-sensor satellite data analysis using Landsat, Sentinel-1 SAR, and other platforms. From change detection to habitat monitoring to accuracy assessment — I can design and execute the full analysis pipeline.

🌍 Google Earth Engine Development Custom GEE scripts, applications, and workflows for large-scale geospatial analysis. I have built production-ready GEE applications including the Belize EcoDynamics Explorer and the forthcoming Belize EcoServices Portal.

💧 Ecosystem Service Assessment Spatial modeling and valuation of ecosystem services including carbon storage, coastal protection, water quality regulation, and biodiversity support. Informed by peer-reviewed methodology and designed for real-world policy applications.

📊 Geospatial Data Science End-to-end data science workflows using R, Python, and GIS platforms including ArcGIS and QGIS. From raw data to publication-ready visualizations and reproducible analysis pipelines.

Pricing is project-dependent. Contact me to discuss your needs.

🎤 Workshops#

I design and deliver workshops that make technical geospatial tools accessible to researchers, practitioners, and students at all levels. Workshops can be delivered in person or remotely and are tailored to your audience and goals.

Current Workshop Offerings#

Introduction to Google Earth Engine A hands-on introduction to GEE for researchers and practitioners with no prior experience. Covers the code editor, basic remote sensing concepts, and building your first analysis workflow. Suitable for: Beginners, conservation practitioners, graduate students

Remote Sensing for Conservation A practical workshop connecting remote sensing methods to real-world conservation monitoring and planning applications. Suitable for: NGO staff, conservation biologists, land managers

Ecosystem Mapping and Classification A technical workshop covering land cover classification methods, accuracy assessment, and reproducible mapping workflows in GEE and R. Suitable for: Graduate students, researchers, GIS professionals

Introduction to GIS for Environmental Science A beginner-friendly introduction to GIS concepts and tools using QGIS. Suitable for: Undergraduates, early-career researchers, NGO staff

Custom workshops available on request. Let’s talk about what your team needs.
