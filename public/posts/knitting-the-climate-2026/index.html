What Is A Temperature Blanket?#

Every day I knit one row based on the temperature outside.

Each color tells a story:

| Temperature (°F) | Color |

|—————–|——-|

| 100°F+ | ⬛ Black |

| 90–100°F | 🟪 Pink |

| 80–90°F | 🟥 Red |

| 70–80°F | 🟧 Orange |

| 60–70°F | 🟨 Yellow |

| 50–60°F | 🟩 Green |

| 40–50°F | 🔵 Light Blue |

| 30–40°F | 🔵 Dark Blue |

| 20–30°F | 🟣 Purple |

| 10–20°F | ⬜ Gray |

| Month Start | ⬜ White |

Why I Do This#

As a climate scientist I spend my days working with temperature

data in spreadsheets and satellite imagery. The temperature blanket

is my way of making that data tangible — something I can hold,

something that grows with the year, something that tells the story

of climate in a way that a graph never quite can.

Every unusual color is a conversation starter. Every long stretch

of red is a reminder of why this work matters.
