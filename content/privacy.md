## 🔒 Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 2026*

---

### Information We Collect
When you submit a data request through this website we collect your 
name, institution, email address, country, and intended use of the data. 
We also collect optional information about how you found this resource 
and your communication preferences.

---

### How We Use Your Information
Your information is used solely to:
- Process your data request and send download links
- Send research updates and communications you have explicitly 
  opted into
- Track data usage for academic impact reporting

---

### What We Do Not Do
- We do not sell or share your information with third parties
- We do not use your information for commercial purposes
- We do not contact you beyond what you have opted into

---
