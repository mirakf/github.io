## 👋 Hi, I'm Mira!

**Ecosystems are disappearing faster than we can map them... and the 
services they provide to people are disappearing with them.**


As a PhD Candidate in the Department of Earth and Environment at Boston 
University, my goal is to comprehend what we have before we lose it. I am 
a biogeographer interested in understanding how anthropogenic climate change 
and urbanization affect ecosystem distribution and their associated services. 
Using a combination of satellite remote sensing, data science, and 
ground-truth validation, I established an annual ecosystem map of Belize 
spanning 2015–2026 with 85% accuracy. My current research investigates how 
landscape dynamics, such as adjacency, proximity, and fragmentation, 
affect the services these ecosystems provide to people locally and globally. 
Ultimately, my work is about understanding what we stand to lose, and 
giving communities and conservationists the tools to act before it is 
too late.

My PhD is supported by the [**NSF-Strong Coasts Grant**](https://www.facebook.com/StrongCoastsCoPe/), reflecting 
the broader coastal resilience context of my work in Belize.

## 🌿 Fieldwork

In February 2026, my team from Boston University joined forces with 
[Wild Earth Allies](https://wildearthallies.org/) to ground-truth 
ecosystems around Placencia, Belize. We travelled by boat through riparian forests, hiked 
submontane forests, and confirmed a dozen distinct forest types with 
some of the best botanists and ecologists in the region.

## 🐦 Beyond Research

My passion for ecosystems goes well beyond what you can see on the screen! 
I have partnered with the New England Aquarium as a whale watch naturalist since 2015. 
Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary is world reknowned as a feeding ground for humpback whales. See recent sightings and
photos by my colleagues: **[@whalesofstellwagen](https://www.instagram.com/whalesofstellwagen/)**

I am also an avid birder and active member of the 
**[Brookline Bird Club](https://www.brooklinebirdclub.org/)** and the 
**[Essex County Ornithological Society](https://essexcountybirds.org/)**. 
Birding is something that not only gets me out of the office but reminds me why I do this work. You can follow 
my sightings on **[eBird](https://ebird.org/profile/Mzg4NDUwNA?continue)**.
