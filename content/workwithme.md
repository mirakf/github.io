## 🛠️ Consulting Services

My research expertise translates directly into practical skills for 
organizations working at the intersection of conservation, data science, 
and environmental planning. I offer consulting services to NGOs, 
government agencies, academic institutions, and private organizations.
.
### What I Offer

**🗺️ Custom Ecosystem and Land Cover Mapping**
Reproducible, satellite-derived ecosystem maps tailored to your study 
area and conservation planning needs. My work in Belize demonstrates 
this capability at the national scale across 27 ecosystem types and 
11 years of annual data. See the [Belize Ecosystem Atlas](https://mirakf.github.io/belize-ecosystem-atlas/) for more information

**📡 Remote Sensing Analysis**
Multi-sensor satellite data analysis using Landsat, Sentinel-2, Sentinel-1, 
and other sensots. I can design and execute a full analysis 
pipeline for change detection, habitat monitoring, and 
accuracy assessments.

**🌍 Google Earth Engine Development**
Custom GEE scripts, applications, and workflows for large-scale 
geospatial analysis. I have built production-ready GEE applications 
including the 
[Belize EcoDynamics Explorer](https://ee-mirakf-s25.projects.earthengine.app/view/belize-ecosystems) 
and the forthcoming Belize EcoServices Portal.

**💧 Ecosystem Service Assessment**
Spatial modeling and valuation of ecosystem services including carbon 
storage, coastal protection, water quality regulation, and biodiversity 
support. I specialize in working within the Multiscale Integrated Model of Ecosystem Services (MIMES) framework.

**📊 Geospatial Data Science**
End-to-end data science workflows using R, Python, and GIS platforms 
including ArcGIS and QGIS. From raw data to publication-ready 
visualizations and reproducible analysis pipelines.

*Pricing is project-dependent. 
[Contact me](mailto:mirakf@bu.edu) to discuss your needs.*

## 🎤 Workshops

I design and deliver workshops that make technical geospatial tools 
accessible to researchers, practitioners, and students at all levels. 
Workshops can be delivered in person or remotely and are tailored 
to your audience and goals.

### Current Workshop Offerings

**Introduction to Google Earth Engine**
A hands-on introduction to GEE for researchers and practitioners with 
no prior experience. Covers the code editor, basic remote sensing 
concepts, and building your first analysis workflow.
*Suitable for: Beginners, conservation practitioners, graduate students*

**Remote Sensing for Conservation**
A practical workshop connecting remote sensing methods to real-world 
conservation monitoring and planning applications.
*Suitable for: NGO staff, conservation biologists, land managers*

**Ecosystem Mapping and Classification**
A technical workshop covering land cover classification methods, 
accuracy assessment, and reproducible mapping workflows in GEE and R.
*Suitable for: Graduate students, researchers, GIS professionals*

**Introduction to GIS for Environmental Science**
A beginner-friendly introduction to GIS concepts and tools using QGIS.
*Suitable for: Undergraduates, early-career researchers, NGO staff*
