My publications represent the breadth of my research interests, from the coastal sediments in the Gulf of Maine
to the ecosystem valuation of Caribbean mangroves. Overall I seek to answer the core question:
how do we measure, map, and protect what nature provides to people?

---

## 📄 Peer-Reviewed Publications

**[Mapping Knowledge and Stakeholder Engagement in Mangrove Ecosystem 
Service Valuation: Insights from a Bibliometric Analysis of the Caribbean 
and the Gulf of Mexico](https://doi.org/10.3390/jmse14030259)**  
Kelly-Fair, M., et al. (2026)  
*Journal of Marine Science and Engineering*

💡 A systematic bibliometric analysis examining the critical knowledge gaps in
ecosystem service valuations for mangrove forests in the Caribbean and Gulf of Mexico.

*🌊 Coastal & Marine · 🌿 Ecosystem Services · ⭐ First Author*

---

**[Dynamic Integrated Modeling of Coastal & Estuarine Ecosystem 
Services](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/treatise-on-estuarine-and-coastal-science/baird/978-0-323-90798-9)**  
Boumans, R., et al. (2024)  
*Treatise on Estuarine and Coastal Science, 2nd Edition*

💡 A book chapter presenting integrated modeling frameworks for 
coastal and estuarine ecosystems focused on service supplies.

*🌊 Coastal & Marine · 🌿 Ecosystem Services · 📚 Book Chapter*

---

**[Distributive Energy Justice: Who Lost Power in Texas During the 
2021 Winter Storm?](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.erss.2024.103416)**  
Kaufmann, R., et al. (2024)  
*Energy Research & Social Science*

💡 An analysis of the 2021 Texas winter storm power outages through an 
equity lens, revealing which communities bore the greatest burden of 
infrastructure failure.

*🏙️ Urban & Policy · ⚡ Energy Justice*

---

**[Predicting Spring Phenology in Deciduous Broadleaf Forests: NEON 
Phenology Forecasting Community Challenge](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agrformet.2023.109810)**  
Wheeler, K., et al. (2024)  
*Agricultural and Forest Meteorology*

💡 A large community forecasting challenge using NEON data to predict 
spring leaf-out timing across deciduous forests, comparing approaches 
from dozens of research groups nationwide.

*🌱 Ecological Forecasting · 🌿 Biodiversity*

---

**[Analysis of Land Use and Land Cover Changes through the Lens of SDGs 
in Semarang, Indonesia](https://doi.org/10.3390/su14137592)**  
Kelly-Fair, M., et al. (2022)  
*Sustainability*

💡 Tracking two decades of land use change in a rapidly urbanizing 
Indonesian coastal city and evaluating progress toward the UN Sustainable 
Development Goals.

*🏙️ Land Use & Urban Change · 🗺️ Remote Sensing · ⭐ First Author*

---

**[Assessing Sediment Budgets in Support of Beach Nourishment and 
Coastal Community Resiliency](https://digitalmaine.com/geo_docs/147)**  
Dickson, S.M., et al. (2020)  
*Geology Documents*

💡 A sediment budget assessment informing beach nourishment strategies 
for coastal communities in Maine facing erosion and rising seas.

*🌊 Coastal & Marine*

---

**[Comparative Study of Terrestrial Arthropod Diversity in Primary and 
Re-planted Pine Forest in a Community Forest at Andasibe, 
Madagascar](https://digitalcollections.sit.edu/isp_collection/2174/)**  
George, T., et al. (2015)  
*Independent Study Project (ISP) Collection*

💡 A field-based study comparing arthropod communities in primary versus 
replanted forest in Madagascar with a focus on
biodiversity recovery in restored ecosystems.

*🌿 Biodiversity*
